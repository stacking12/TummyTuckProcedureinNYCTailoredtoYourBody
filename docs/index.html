About Our Tummy Tuck Procedure

A tummy tuck procedure removes excess skin and fat from the abdomen and, when needed, tightens the stretched muscles underneath. It is a common choice after pregnancy, weight loss, or years of changes that diet and exercise cannot fully reverse.

At Extended & Mini Tummy Tuck in New York City, we focus on two approaches: the mini and the extended technique. Each plan begins with a close look at your skin, muscle tone, and goals, so the result looks natural and proportionate.

Tummy Tuck Procedure FAQs

What is the difference between a mini and an extended tummy tuck?

A mini tummy tuck focuses on the area below the belly button and uses a shorter incision. An extended tummy tuck treats the full abdomen and also the flanks, so it suits people with more loose skin that wraps toward the sides.

Am I a good candidate for a tummy tuck procedure?

Good candidates are generally healthy adults at a stable weight who are bothered by loose skin or a stretched abdominal wall that diet and exercise have not changed. Your surgeon confirms candidacy during an in-person consultation.

How long is the recovery after tummy tuck surgery?

Many people return to light daily activity within one to two weeks, though recovery varies by procedure type and by person. Strenuous exercise is usually delayed for several weeks. Your surgeon gives you a personal timeline.

Will a tummy tuck leave a scar?

Yes. Every tummy tuck leaves a scar. Incisions are planned low so they can be hidden by underwear or swimwear, and scars fade gradually over time. Mini tummy tucks leave a shorter scar than extended ones.
